Note: {{parameters}}

Load the `note` skill and follow its workflow.

Write a new note to `docs/notes/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>.md`. Include:

1. **Title** -- short and specific
2. **Context** -- why this note exists
3. **Content** -- the actual information, decision, or learning
4. **References** -- files, URLs, or related notes (cited with `path:line` where applicable)

Keep it tight. Notes are for future-you, not for theatre.
